# Procurement AI – AI-Powered Procurement Assistant

## 1. Project Overview

**Procurement AI** is an Artificial Intelligence-powered procurement assistant designed to improve purchasing decisions, optimize supplier selection, and increase efficiency in procurement operations.

The project aims to support procurement professionals by using AI technologies to identify unknown spare parts, find equivalent products, compare technical specifications, evaluate suppliers, and recommend the most suitable purchasing options.

Instead of relying solely on manual research and individual experience, Procurement AI uses AI-driven analysis to provide structured information and decision support.

**The main objective is to make procurement processes smarter, faster, more transparent, and more cost-effective while maintaining human control over final decisions.**

## 2. Key Features

### 2.1. Spare Parts Identification
- Identify unknown industrial spare parts using product codes, model numbers, labels, and technical descriptions.
- Recognize manufacturers and product categories.
- Analyze technical information from product datasheets and documentation.
- Help identify discontinued or obsolete components.

### 2.2. Alternative Product Identification
- Find alternative products from different manufacturers.
- Identify direct replacements and compatible alternatives.
- Compare original products with proposed equivalents.
- Evaluate technical compatibility and potential limitations.
- Highlight differences that may affect equipment performance or reliability.

### 2.3. Technical Comparison
- Compare product specifications and technical parameters.
- Analyze dimensions, materials, operating pressure, voltage, capacity, and other relevant characteristics.
- Identify similarities and differences between products.
- Highlight technical risks and compatibility issues.

### 2.4. Supplier and Price Evaluation
- Compare quotations from multiple suppliers.
- Evaluate product prices and commercial conditions.
- Analyze supplier reliability and available information.
- Compare offers based on total procurement cost, not just the initial purchase price.

### 2.5. Delivery Time Evaluation

**Delivery time is a critical factor in procurement decision-making.**

Procurement AI considers delivery lead time alongside price, technical compatibility, and supplier reliability.

The system aims to:
- Compare delivery times offered by different suppliers.
- Identify the fastest available supply options.
- Evaluate the impact of delivery delays on production and maintenance activities.
- Consider delivery time when ranking alternative products and supplier offers.
- Highlight situations where a higher-priced option may be more advantageous due to significantly faster delivery.

### 2.6. Intelligent Decision Support
- Rank suppliers and alternative products according to defined evaluation criteria.
- Provide recommendations based on technical, commercial, and logistical considerations.
- Explain the advantages and disadvantages of each option.
- Identify potential procurement risks.
- Generate structured comparison reports to support purchasing decisions.

## 3. Evaluation Criteria

Procurement AI evaluates purchasing options using several key criteria:

| Evaluation Criteria | Description |
|---|---|
| Technical Compatibility | Suitability of the product for the required application |
| Price | Purchase price and overall cost |
| Delivery Time | Estimated lead time and availability |
| Supplier Reliability | Supplier reputation and performance |
| Product Quality | Manufacturer standards and product specifications |
| Warranty & Support | Warranty conditions and technical assistance |
| Logistics | Transportation costs and delivery conditions |

The importance of each criterion can vary depending on the urgency and technical requirements of the procurement request.

## 4. Human Oversight and Decision-Making

Procurement AI is designed as a **decision-support system, not a replacement for procurement professionals.**

AI-generated information and recommendations must be reviewed and validated by a human expert before making final purchasing decisions.

Human oversight is particularly important for:
- Confirming technical compatibility.
- Verifying supplier information and quotations.
- Assessing operational and production risks.
- Approving alternative products.
- Making final supplier selection and purchasing decisions.

The final decision always remains with the responsible procurement professional.

## 5. Expected Benefits

- Reduced time spent on technical research.
- Faster identification of unknown spare parts.
- Improved identification of equivalent products.
- More accurate technical and commercial comparisons.
- Better supplier selection.
- Reduced procurement costs.
- Improved delivery-time management.
- Lower risk of purchasing incompatible components.
- More transparent and data-driven procurement decisions.
- Reduced dependency on manual research.

## 6. Potential Technologies

The project may incorporate the following technologies:

- **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** Intelligent analysis and recommendations.
- **Large Language Models (LLMs):** Understanding technical descriptions and supplier quotations.
- **Machine Learning (ML):** Supplier evaluation and procurement data analysis.
- **Web Search and Data Extraction:** Finding product information and supplier offers.
- **Python:** Data processing and automation.
- **Excel / CSV Integration:** Analyzing procurement data and quotations.
- **Database Management:** Storing product specifications, supplier information, and historical purchasing data.

*The final technology stack will depend on the project's development stage and implementation requirements.*

## 7. Future Development

Potential future improvements include:

1. Automated quotation analysis from PDF, Excel, and email attachments.
2. Integration with ERP and procurement management systems.
3. Historical supplier performance analysis.
4. Automated RFQ preparation and supplier communication.
5. AI-based supplier risk assessment.
6. Spare parts database with verified technical equivalents.
7. Predictive analysis of spare parts requirements.
8. Automated procurement reporting and dashboards.
9. Multi-language support (English, Azerbaijani, Turkish, and Russian).

## 8. Project Vision

The long-term vision of Procurement AI is to develop an intelligent procurement platform that combines technical expertise, commercial analysis, supplier intelligence, and logistics evaluation in one system.

By combining artificial intelligence with professional human oversight, the project aims to transform traditional procurement into a more efficient, reliable, and data-driven business process.
